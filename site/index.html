xcpredict

Predicting FIS Cross-Country World Cup results from what each skier has done in races like this one — same technique, similar distance, recently. Pick any race below to see what the model would have said before the start, next to what actually happened.

How it works. Every past result is weighted by how much it resembles the race being predicted — technique, sprint or distance, distance in km, and how long ago. A 10 km skate leans on other 10 km skates, then 15 km skates, then 10 km classics, and barely on sprints. Nothing is thrown away; the weights just differ. A small ranking model is then fitted on those summaries.

Nothing here uses the result it is predicting. A race's prediction is built only from races that finished before it. Seasons marked held out were excluded from training entirely.

Source: github.com/deknapp/xcpredict. Data: FIS.
